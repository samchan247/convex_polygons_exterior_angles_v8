1. Anatomy of a Convex Polygon

An n-sided convex polygon is a closed figure with n straight sides where every interior angle is strictly less than 180°.

  • Convexity All vertices point outward; every diagonal lies completely inside
  • Interior ∠ (i) Angle formed inside the polygon by two adjacent sides
  • Exterior ∠ (e) Angle between one side and the extension of its adjacent side (i + e = 180°)
  • Sides & Vertices n straight sides, n interior angles, and n exterior angles
